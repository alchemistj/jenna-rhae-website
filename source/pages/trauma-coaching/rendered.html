What We Do

What Does Trauma Recovery Coaching Include?

Trauma affects every woman differently — which is why healing here is never one-size-fits-all. Coaching with Jenna is built around your nervous system, your story, and your faith. We move at the pace your body can hold, not a predetermined program timeline.

  • Survival pattern identification and healing
  • Nervous system regulation practices
  • Hypervigilance and emotional flooding support
  • Identity rebuilding after trauma
  • Boundary development — from a place of stewardship, not selfishness
  • Grief and loss processing
  • Spiritual conflict healing (faith vs. self-abandonment)
  • Rest and safety rebuilding
  • Shame deconstruction
  • Purpose and identity clarification
  • Faith reintegration for women with church hurt or spiritual numbness

How Much Does Trauma Carry Over Into Daily Life?

For many women, trauma's most persistent effect isn't a dramatic flashback — it's the low-grade exhaustion of always being “on.” Racing thoughts, difficulty sleeping, tension in the body, guilt around rest, over-responsibility, and the quiet feeling that something is always about to go wrong. These are survival responses — not character flaws.

When your nervous system learned that alertness was protection, peace can feel unfamiliar — even unsafe. That's not weakness. That's an adaptive response doing exactly what it was designed to do. The work of trauma recovery coaching is gently expanding your window of tolerance, so that safety starts to feel like the norm rather than the exception.

What Is Faith-Integrated Trauma Coaching?

Faith-integrated trauma coaching holds space for both your nervous system and your relationship with God — without asking you to choose between them. For many women in survival mode, there is deep spiritual conflict: Is rest selfish? Am I failing God? Is this trauma — or is this obedience? These are real questions, and they deserve real answers.

Jenna's approach draws from both trauma-informed coaching methodology and Biblical counseling — helping you discern what God actually asked you to carry, and gently lay down what survival taught you to hold. Healing here is spiritually congruent, not spiritually pressured.

How Long Does Trauma Recovery Coaching Take?

There is no honest universal answer — and Jenna will never give you one. Healing is not linear, and the timeline depends entirely on where you are, what you are carrying, and how gently your nervous system needs to move. Some women experience significant shifts within a few sessions. Others engage in longer-term coaching over months.

What Jenna can promise: you will never be rushed. This work is not performance-based. Your pace is the right pace.

Is Trauma Recovery Coaching the Same as Therapy?

No — and the distinction matters. Trauma recovery coaching is a forward-focused process that helps you identify patterns, build practical tools, and move toward healing. It does not diagnose or treat clinical mental health conditions. Jenna is a Certified Biblical Counselor and Emotional Freedom Life Coach — not a licensed therapist.

If clinical care is what you need, Jenna will tell you directly and help you find the right support. For many women, coaching and therapy work well together — and Jenna is comfortable working alongside your existing mental health team.

FAQ

Common Questions About Trauma Recovery Coaching

Can coaching help with complex or childhood trauma?

Coaching can be a valuable complement to trauma healing work, particularly around identity, patterns, and faith integration. Jenna works with women navigating the downstream effects of complex and childhood trauma — the survival patterns, identity confusion, and spiritual conflict that often persist long after the original wound. If clinical trauma treatment is needed, she will refer you to the appropriate care.

What if I am not sure whether I have trauma?

Many women in survival mode do not identify as “traumatized” — they identify as exhausted, anxious, over-responsible, or spiritually disconnected. If you feel stuck in patterns you cannot explain, rest feels unsafe, or you are carrying more than you can name, a free consultation call is the right first step.

Will we talk about God and faith in every session?

Faith is woven into the framework, not forced into every conversation. Sessions follow your lead — some will be deeply spiritual, others more practical and grounded. You will never be required to frame your healing in theological terms, but the option is always available.

How do I know if I am ready for coaching?

You do not need to be ready — you need to be willing. Coaching meets you where you are, not where you think you should be. If you are curious whether this is the right fit, the free consultation call exists precisely for that question.

What does a typical session look like?

Sessions are typically conducted via video call, run approximately 45-60 minutes, and are structured around your current goals, current barriers, and what your nervous system needs most in that window. Jenna does not use a rigid script — sessions are responsive, not formulaic.
